## About TalkWave

TalkWave is an open-source, self-hostable platform for AI voice agents. Design call flows in a visual editor, connect the LLM, speech-to-text, and text-to-speech providers you already use, and take calls over the phone, in the browser, or in online meetings.

- **Open source** under the BSD 2-Clause license, with no usage limits.
- **Self-hosted**: your calls, recordings, and keys stay on your infrastructure.
- **Bring your own providers** for models, speech, and telephony.

## Quick start

You need [Docker](https://docs.docker.com/get-docker/) with Compose v2.24 or newer and a free [Clerk](https://dashboard.clerk.com) development instance for sign-in.

```bash
git clone https://github.com/TalhaaaYaqoob/talkwave.git
cd talkwave
cp .env.example .env
cp api/talkwave-backend/env.template api/talkwave-backend/.env
docker compose up --build
```

Fill in the passwords and the Clerk publishable key in `.env`, then add `CLERK_SECRET_KEY` and at least one LLM, speech-to-text, and text-to-speech key in `api/talkwave-backend/.env`.

Open [http://localhost:3010](http://localhost:3010), sign in, and create your first agent.
